Airport Guide

Tips for a Stress-Free Airport Transfer

A little preparation can make the start or end of a trip feel effortless. Use this practical guide to plan your airport pick-up, keep your journey on track, and arrive ready for what comes next.

March 2026 5 min read Citilinkers Travel Journal

Plan the details before travel day

Airports are busy, and even a straightforward transfer involves a few moving parts. Booking your ride in advance gives you time to share the right pick-up point, confirm the number of passengers, and choose a vehicle with enough room for everyone and their luggage.

Before you confirm, check your flight details, arrival terminal, destination address, and contact number. If your plans change, update your transfer provider as early as possible so the driver has the latest information.

Share your flight number

When arranging an airport pick-up, include the airline and flight number rather than relying on an estimated landing time alone. Flight details help the dispatch team follow arrival updates and coordinate the collection around your actual flight schedule.

Allow time to clear the airport

Landing is only one part of your arrival. Passport control, baggage collection, and the walk to the meeting point can all take time, particularly at larger terminals or during busy periods. When booking, make sure your collection plan accounts for these steps.

Keep your phone switched on after landing and check your booking confirmation for the agreed meeting point and driver contact instructions.

Choose a vehicle that fits your group

Passenger count is only part of the picture. Consider the size and number of suitcases, pushchairs, or other items you are bringing. A saloon may suit a smaller party with light luggage, while a larger group or an extended stay may call for an estate or MPV.

  • Count passengers and bags before selecting a vehicle.
  • Request child or booster seats when you make your booking.
  • Tell the provider about oversized or specialist luggage in advance.

Know where to meet your driver

Check whether your booking is for a kerbside collection or a meet-and-greet inside arrivals. Make sure you know which terminal and meeting point to use, and keep your booking reference available. Clear instructions make it easier for everyone to connect, even when the terminal is crowded.

A simple final check

The day before you travel, review your confirmation and make sure your flight, pick-up time, destination, passenger details, and luggage requirements are correct. With the essentials taken care of, you can focus on the trip instead of the logistics.
