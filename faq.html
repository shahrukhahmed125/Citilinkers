Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, airport pick-ups, vehicle options and payment methods.

Booking & Fares

Use our online fare calculator on the homepage. Input your pick-up point, destination, date and time to choose your vehicle category and receive a guaranteed quote.
All Citilinkers quotes are 100% fixed guaranteed prices. Toll fees and airport drop-off zone charges are transparently calculated upfront with no surge pricing.
You can amend your travel date, time, or passenger details up to 12 hours prior to dispatch by logging into your My Account portal or contacting our 24/7 dispatch desk.

Airport Pickup & Flight Delays

Your driver will be waiting in the arrivals hall immediately after passport control and baggage claim holding a prominent sign with your name or company title.
When you supply your flight number during booking, our dispatch radar tracks your aircraft in real time. Driver dispatch times are automatically adjusted to match your actual landing time at no extra fee.

Payment & Fleet

We accept major debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) as well as direct corporate invoicing for corporate account holders.
